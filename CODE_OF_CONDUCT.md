# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behaviour include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Project maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or revert contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
