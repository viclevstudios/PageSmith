# PageSmith PDF — UI direction

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

PageSmith PDF is presented as a focused local desktop utility: a quiet workbench for choosing files, checking the relevant output option, selecting a destination, and creating the result. The interface should feel precise and trustworthy through density, hierarchy, and state clarity rather than decoration.

## Visual language

- Dark layered surfaces: application background, sidebar, workspace, and controls are visibly distinct without gradients or glass effects.
- Blue is reserved for focus, selection, and the primary action; green communicates completion or success; red is reserved for errors.
- System-adjacent sans-serif typography with a compact scale, restrained weights, and clear labels.
- Small, consistent radii and thin borders only where they clarify a boundary or an interactive affordance.
- The workflow is organized around the file drop/select area and a sticky, clearly separated action zone.

## Interaction principles

- Files are the first and most visually prominent decision.
- Options are compact and become visually stronger once files exist.
- The destination and primary action stay findable in the normal viewport.
- Empty, dragging, selected, processing, success, and error states use structure, copy, and iconography as well as color.
- Keyboard focus, custom select navigation, and smaller windows are first-class behavior.

## Constraints

Preserve all existing copy, tool behavior, bilingual support, local processing, and PDF-processing logic. Do not add claims, marketing sections, decorative gradients, or a card for every piece of information.
